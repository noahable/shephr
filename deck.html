The Problem
"Ops teams are the only comparable high-consequence role in aviation without mandatory simulation. Every other critical function trains on simulators. Operations teams don't."

The incumbent vendor category — Sabre, Amadeus, Lufthansa Systems — has solved for recovery speed and optimisation. None of them have solved for the decision quality of the humans running those tools. The gap is structural, and it costs airlines on every significant disruption event.

Gap 01
The intelligence gap
Connection clusters, fragility scores, and cascade flags are scattered across disconnected systems. Passengers on partner metal are invisible. The window to act closes before the full picture arrives.
Unaddressed by incumbents
Gap 02
The decision quality gap
Having a dashboard and having trained instinct are different things. No existing tool builds the decision muscle memory that turns information into correct action under time pressure and simultaneous constraint collision.
Unaddressed by incumbents
Gap 03 — New · April 2026
The legal exposure gap
Under T-656/24, voluntarily holding a flight can become the determining cause of the delay — voiding the EU261 defence on that and downstream rotations. No current tool calculates this before the ops team acts.
Shepherd addresses all three
